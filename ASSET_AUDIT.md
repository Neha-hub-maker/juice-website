## Findings

- No corrupt files, duplicate ID matches or ID/type mismatches were found. All output videos passed full decoding, H.264/yuv420p/no-audio checks and moov-before-mdat faststart validation. The scrub clip was checked frame by frame: every frame is a keyframe.
- **.keep:** Repository marker; not media.
- **Gemini_Generated_Image_bgnuc5bgnuc5bgnu.jpg:** Landscape lemonade image; the other flavor images are square. Choose card crops deliberately.
- **Orange_juice_splashing_from_glass_20261013024159.gif:** Low resolution; keep native dimensions and do not upscale.
- **Orange_juice_splashing_from_glass_20261013024159.gif:** Animated GIF, not a still image as described in README; converted to MP4 and poster.
- No supplied video contains the complete DNA helix formation and continuous resolution into a glass. The helix still is present; the splash clips are separate footage.
- Pexels gallery images are optional and stylistically different. Retain source credits and confirm applicable license terms before publication.
- No original exceeds 50 MB. Keep assets committed; /assets was not added to .gitignore.
- WebP quality starts at 80 and decreases only if a size budget requires it. Backgrounds are at most 1600px wide; cards and optional gallery images at most 900px. Sources are never upscaled.
- Regular videos are at most 1280px wide and under 4 MB. Encoding starts at CRF 26 and increases CRF (reduces quality) or decreases resolution if needed. The separate scrub clip is exactly 960px wide with -g 1.
